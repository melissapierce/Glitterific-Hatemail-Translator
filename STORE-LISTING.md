# Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** Glitterific Hate Mail Translator

**Summary (132 characters max):**
Turn hate mail into fan mail with one click. Swaps hostile words for sparkly ones in Gmail and Outlook, all inside your browser.

**Category:** Communication, or Well-being if the dashboard offers it

**Description:**

Are you tired of poring over the same old boring, misogynistic emails and hate-filled comments from internet trolls? With the Glitterific Hate Mail Translator, your inbox and comment sections can be as fresh and sparkly as you are.

Glitterific swaps hostile words for glittery ones as you read. "Nobody cares, you pathetic loser" becomes "Everybody cares, you poetic legend." It works on its own in Gmail and Outlook on the web, and on any other page when you click Glitter this page.

NEW IN 2026: Glitterific is back, rebuilt from scratch for today's Chrome.

• See what they said. Click any glittered word to see the original, or turn on Show what they said to see every original word, struck through.
• Threats stay visible. If a message looks like a threat, Glitterific leaves it exactly as it was sent and points you to Right To Be's guide to responding to online harassment, so you can save it and report it.
• Your replies stay yours. Nothing you type is ever changed.
• Private by design. Everything happens in your browser, and Glitterific collects nothing.

Glitterific won't stop online harassment. It offers another way to sit with it: lighter, a little absurd, and on your own terms.

First released in 2013 and inspired by Danielle Sucher's Jailbreak the Patriarchy. Revived in 2026 by Melissa Pierce, built with Claude.

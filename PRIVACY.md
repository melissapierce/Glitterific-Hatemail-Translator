# Glitterific Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Glitterific Hate Mail Translator runs entirely in your browser.

It reads the text of the pages it glitters (Gmail, Outlook on the web, and any page where you click Glitter this page) only to swap words on your screen. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share that text or any personal information.

The only things Glitterific saves are your two settings, whether glitter is on and whether Show what they said is on. They're kept in Chrome's built-in extension storage, which Chrome may sync across browsers where you're signed in.

Glitterific has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.
